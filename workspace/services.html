Our Services

Specialized mental healthcare for adults, children, and adolescents

Receive care from highly trained clinicians specializing in today's most prominent mental health issues & evidence-based treatments. We offer in-person therapy in South Tampa and secure virtual therapy throughout Florida.

Individual Therapy

One-on-one therapy tailored to your unique needs, helping you navigate life's challenges and foster personal growth.

Couples Therapy

Strengthen your relationship, improve communication, and work through challenges together using evidence-based approaches.

Child & Adolescent Therapy

Age-appropriate support for children and teens navigating anxiety, depression, behavioral issues, and life transitions.

Family Therapy

Improve family dynamics, communication, and resolve conflicts in a supportive environment.

Maternal Mental Health Therapy

Support through infertility, pregnancy, postpartum, and the emotional journey of motherhood.

Adult ADHD Evaluations

Comprehensive assessments to understand if ADHD is impacting your life and develop strategies that work.

Therapy for Trauma & PTSD

Evidence-based trauma treatments including EMDR, CPT, and PE to help you heal and regain a sense of safety.

Therapy for Anxiety

Learn practical tools to manage anxiety, panic, social anxiety, and OCD using proven therapeutic approaches.

Therapy for Depression

Break free from the heaviness of depression with compassionate support and evidence-based interventions.

Therapy for Stress & Burnout

Reclaim your energy and find balance when stress and burnout have taken over.

Therapy for Disordered Eating & Body Image

Compassionate support for healing your relationship with food, body, and self.

Online Therapy in Florida

Secure, HIPAA-compliant virtual therapy sessions from the comfort and privacy of your home.
